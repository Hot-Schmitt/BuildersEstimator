## Decision

Three screens were locked as sketches on 2026-11-16 and keep their logic and wording:

- **New estimate:** typed client name and site address; a "Job name" field that fills
  itself in as site address, a hyphen, then client name, and stops updating once he
  edits it; then 1 "Job type", 2 "Documents" (with a note on what to upload, and a label
  per document), 3 "Choose template" (one, several to combine, or "Create new"), and
  "Create estimate". An estimate can be created without documents, but reading the plans
  waits and says why.
- **Add vendor or sub pricing:** what was read, "Needs you" first, ready line items
  showing the current figure and the new one, exclusions, and "Apply to estimate" with
  undo.
- **Request pricing:** labelled documents with tick boxes, a searchable "Send to" with
  removable chips, the line items to price, a "pricing needed by" date, and "Draft
  emails".

They were drawn at phone width and still need laptop layouts.

## Why

The owner reviewed each one, gave edits and wording, asked for one more logic and layout
review, and then locked them.

## Considered instead

**The first and second sketches of each.** The first made him do too much, by a design
consult's verdict; the second carried per-job questions and per-line settings that were
dropped (decision 0021).

**"Kind of job", "Plans", "Start from".** Replaced by "Job type", "Documents" and
"Choose template" at his word.

## What it costs us

Locked in logic and wording, not in layout: laptop became the first layout afterwards
(decision 0016), so all three must be redrawn. Filling the address in from the dropped
documents is wanted and postponed. Decisions 0010 and 0011 apply before any more
locking.
