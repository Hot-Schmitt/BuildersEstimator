## Decision

The owner produces the builder's estimates as a service, on his own Claude subscription,
with a small kit of scripts on his own computer. Hosting, and any access of the
builder's own, come later and only if volume needs them.

The owner's Claude account is never shared with the builder, and the builder's access is
never built on it. His own access means his own account or the paid API, decided later.

## Why

The owner charges for doing the estimates for now, so nothing has to be hosted or sold
before the method is proven on real jobs.

## Considered instead

**Hosting the tool, or giving the builder his own access, from the start.** Not refused,
put off: it costs money and work before one estimate has been proven accurate.

## What it costs us

A screen that runs only on the owner's computer cannot be reached by the builder. Where
the editing screen lives, and how he reaches it, is still open. Reading plans and
pricing documents stays a step the owner runs (decision 0027).
