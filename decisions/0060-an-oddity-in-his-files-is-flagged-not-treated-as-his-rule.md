## Decision

The builder's estimates and cost sheets are made by hand, so they can hold slips,
missing numbers and rough rounding. An oddity found in them is something to flag and ask
him about, not a rule to copy.

## Why

The owner's example: his paint line is split half interior, half exterior, most likely
because his sub priced the whole house and he halved it so each cost code had a figure.
Read as a rule, that would be wrong on every future job.

## Considered instead

**Treating every pattern in his one sheet as his method.** The natural mistake with a
single job to learn from.

## What it costs us

More questions for him, and slower learning: nothing found on one job becomes a default
until he confirms it or a second job shows it again. Slips found on Job A so far: a
cabinet install listed but not counted, tape not carried to his budget, paint halved
across two codes.

## His words

> it's likely that some of the stuff that you see in his documents or Excel sheets have
> errors, missing numbers, rounding... he just did simple math to divide it, so it had a
> place to go
