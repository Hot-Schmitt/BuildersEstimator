## Decision

Contingency stays as the builder's template has it. It is edited in the cost summary,
by group (for example "applies to Framing materials"). There is no contingency switch
on line items.

## Why

His sheet already carries contingency on groups of lines, so this matches how he works.
A switch on every line item would make the estimate screen busier for no gain.

## Considered instead

**A contingency chip on every line item, matching the Tax chip.** Withdrawn by Claude
before it reached the owner. A second switch on each line item is busy, and contingency
by group does the same work.

## What it costs us

He cannot put contingency on one odd line item without making or changing a group.
Which groups carry contingency as a standing habit is a fact only he knows; it is an
open question for him.

## His words

> Lock 1 to 7

This was lock 3 of seven put to him together on 2026-11-18. The standard he set for all of them, the same day:
