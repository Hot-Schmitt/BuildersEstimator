## Decision

The finished output is a modern, premium on-screen estimate. Excel and CSV are export
options, made on request. The builder's Excel files are source material for building the
estimate, not the thing he receives.

## Why

The owner asked for it in the words below. The method's step 8 ("the draft he receives")
was changed to match the same day.

## Considered instead

**A draft in his own Excel format.** The original plan (decision 0014). Dropped on
2026-11-16 because the owner wanted something more modern and premium than his
spreadsheet, with the spreadsheet still available as an export.

## What it costs us

Screens have to be designed, built and reviewed before he can use anything, where a
spreadsheet draft needed none of that. The estimate must not end up feeling like the
spreadsheet it replaced (decision 0044).

## His words

> the output needs to be a little bit more modern and premium. He can export as an Excel
> or CSV as an option

On the method's step 8:

> yes = update it to the on-screen estimate... lock 1
