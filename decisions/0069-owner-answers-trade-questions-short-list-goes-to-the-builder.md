## Decision

The owner is a builder himself. He answers the questions that are general trade practice.
The agent prepares a short list of the questions only this builder can answer and that
block design work; it goes to the builder with the owner's OK.

The owner's input on what builders need counts as first-hand evidence, beside the market
research. Where the two disagree, it is flagged, not quietly settled. He is not used as
the blind reader of mock screens, because he has seen every sketch.

## Why

`docs/builder/questions.md` holds 48 questions and no answers, and decisions 0002 to 0004
(markup, tax, contingency) rest on one 2022 sheet. The repository audit called the
builder's answers the hidden critical path: screens locked before he answers are the
likeliest thing to be redone.

## Considered instead

**Waiting for the builder to answer all 48.** Slow, and many are not about his personal
habits.

**Treating the owner's answers as the builder's.** Not done: a fact only this builder
knows stays a question for him, and an answer from the owner is recorded as the owner's.

## What it costs us

Some of the owner's time and some of the builder's. An answer that is right for builders
in general can still be wrong for this one; those answers are marked so they can be
checked with him later.

> i am also a builder so my input and eye will aid us
