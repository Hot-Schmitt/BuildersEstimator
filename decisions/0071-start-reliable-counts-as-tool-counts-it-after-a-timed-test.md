## Decision

Proposed, to be decided in phase 1 when the work reaches this step: line items whose
quantity is a plain count off the plans (windows, doors, fixtures and the like) start as
"the tool counts it" and arrive filled in, where every other line starts as "he enters
it" (decision 0070).

Before any of it is built, a feasibility test is run at that moment: a time comparison,
to see whether pre-filled counts actually save him time against entering them himself
with the tool's count shown beside as a check.

## Why

Counts were the one kind of quantity that matched his exactly on the Job A test (windows,
interior and exterior doors, posts). Filling those in is the cheapest real speed-up the
plans can give.

## Considered instead

**Leaving every line at the default for good.** Safe, but it gives up the part of plan
reading that has been shown to work.

**Building it without a test.** Turned down by the owner: the time comparison comes first.

## What it costs us

The evidence is one job. The agent's own view was to hold this until a second job
confirms the counts. A count that is right on the plans can still be wrong for the job
(a door he reuses, a window the client changed), so a pre-filled count still has to show
where it came from and be one tap to change.

How the timed test is run (what is timed, on which made-up or real job, who does the
entering) is not designed yet.
