## Decision

The estimate screen draws the eye to what truly needs his attention and to what he is
working on, and guides him through building the estimate. It must not feel like a
spreadsheet, even if that is what it is underneath. Icons are used wherever they save
him reading.

## Why

The owner's reaction to the laptop sketch he had locked minutes earlier: nothing stood
out, and everything looked the same.

## Considered instead

**A plain grid of his lines with every value visible at once.** Closest to his own sheet
and the easiest to build; it is the look this decision rules out.

## What it costs us

Guiding the eye means choosing what not to show at first, so some things are a click
away. Blue marking five different things was one early result; emphasis has to be
rationed.

## His words

> there's no way to draw the eye to what truly needs attention or what he's working on
> at the moment

> it guides the user visually through creating the bid. It doesn't look like... Excel on
> steroids
