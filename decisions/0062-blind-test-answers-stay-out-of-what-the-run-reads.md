## Decision

- His answers for a job stay out of every document a blind run reads. Scored figures
  live only in a results document that a blind run does not open.
- A job used to build the price list cannot be used to test prices.
- A one-job note (his "usual line" for a kind of work, for example) is not used on the
  job it came from.
- Job A can no longer give a clean blind test, and its prices were never testable. A
  second finished job is needed for both.

## Why

The first "blind" draft of Job A was not fully blind: documents read at the start of
every chat already held some of his quantities (26 lines, scored apart). The remake then
named, in five questions, the line he had used on that same job. And Job A's estimate is
also the source of the price list.

## Considered instead

**Keeping test answers in the working log and the job facts.** Where they were; every
chat read them.

## What it costs us

Accuracy cannot be re-proven on Job A, so every figure for measured quantities and all
of pricing wait on jobs the builder has yet to send. In this repository, where a job's
answers sit so a blind run cannot open them still has to be laid out (decision 0012).
