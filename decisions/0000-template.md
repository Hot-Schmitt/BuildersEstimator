## Decision

What was decided, in two or three sentences. Specific enough to build from.

## Why

The reason. If the owner gave one, his reason, not a tidied-up version of it.

## Considered instead

What else was on the table and why it lost. If a previous decision was reversed,
say what it was and what changed. This section is why the file exists — a decision
without its rejected alternative is just a rule, and nobody can tell later whether
it was argued or assumed.

## What it costs us

The known downside, limit, or thing this makes harder. Write it even when small.
If there is genuinely none, say "None known."

## His words

> Direct quote, where there is one.

## Links

Related decisions, documents, test results.
