## Decision

Screens are reviewed in two lanes, by two separate reviewers:

- **Usability and logic:** function, logic and button mapping. Is everything where it
  needs to be, and does every control do what its label says?
- **Visual design:** does the screen guide him through building the estimate, draw the
  eye to what matters, and not feel like a spreadsheet?

## Why

The owner asked for it. One reviewer covering everything had passed sketches whose
problems he saw at a glance.

## Considered instead

**One reviewer covering usability and design together.** How the earlier rounds ran.

## What it costs us

Two reviewers a round, each roughly 85,000 to 125,000 tokens by past rounds. The owner
is warned first.

## His words

> you're not using one that looks at the app for usability and functionality, logic,
> button mapping... and then another one that's looking at it from a pure design
> standpoint... it guides the user visually through creating the bid. It doesn't look
> like... Excel on steroids
