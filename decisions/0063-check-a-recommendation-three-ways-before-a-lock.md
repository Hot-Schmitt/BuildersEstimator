## Decision

Before a recommendation goes to the owner for a lock, Claude checks it three ways and
says it did: against how the builder's sheet works (totals, divisions, cost codes);
against what the owner has already asked for; and against what it adds to the surface of
the screen.

## Why

On 2026-11-18 eight recommendations went to the owner for locking, and two rounds of
checking at his request changed five of them and withdrew one: where the tax chip shows,
its starting position, vendor pricing and tax, decimals, Duplicate hidden in a menu
against his earlier ask, and a contingency toggle. They had been written straight from
the conversation.

## Considered instead

**Sending recommendations as drafted and fixing them when he asks for a logic check.**
What happened.

## What it costs us

A little more work before each set of decisions reaches him. Not yet approved; the
current-state document lists it as waiting on the owner.

## His words

None recorded. This is Claude's proposal; the owner has not been asked to approve it.
