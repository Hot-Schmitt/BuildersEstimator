## Decision

The top bar of the estimate screen has:

- a **More** menu holding Job documents, Export and Settings;
- **Duplicate** as a visible small button, not inside the menu;
- a plans status line under the job name;
- **Undo** beside "All changes saved".

## Why

The menu keeps rarely used actions off the surface. Duplicate stays visible because
the owner asked for that on 2026-11-16.

## Considered instead

**Duplicate inside the More menu.** Claude's first recommendation. Changed before the
lock when a check against the owner's earlier asks showed it went against what he had
asked for on 2026-11-16.

## What it costs us

One more button on the top bar than the tidiest layout would have.
