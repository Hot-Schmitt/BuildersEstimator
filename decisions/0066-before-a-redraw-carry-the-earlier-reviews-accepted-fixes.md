## Decision

Before a screen is redrawn, Claude lists the fixes the earlier review had accepted and
carries each one into the new drawing, or says why not.

## Why

The first canvas version dropped things an earlier review had required (what the total
leaves out, the cost summary entry, search), and the usability reviewer had to ask for
them back. They were lost while simplifying for looks.

## Considered instead

**Redrawing from the complaint alone.** What was done.
