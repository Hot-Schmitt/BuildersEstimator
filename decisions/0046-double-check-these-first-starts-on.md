## Decision

"Double-check these first", the list of line items where the money is most at risk,
starts switched on. He can turn the list off, and when he opens it he can switch on or
off which of the tool's checks are included.

## Why

On Job A, plan-count quantities ran 15 to 45% short of what he buys. With confidence
flags off by default (decision 0045), this list is the one place the tool says where the
money is at risk; with it off too, nothing would warn him.

## Considered instead

**Starting it off.** The other option offered, for a calmer first screen.

## What it costs us

One more thing on a screen that is meant to be calm. The five checks behind the list,
their starting switches, the one-year price age and a "Looks right" button were Proposed
and still open in the current-state document.
