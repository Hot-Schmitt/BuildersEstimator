## Decision

When a pricing document carries its own quantities (a lumber list, for example), the
vendor's quantity is shown beside ours, flagged, and he picks which one stands. A
quantity is never swapped silently.

## Why

It follows the hard rule that when two sources disagree we flag it and do not pick one.
A vendor's take-off may well be better than a plan count, but that is his call.

## Considered instead

**Taking the vendor's quantity automatically, or keeping ours automatically.** Either
one swaps or ignores a number without his knowing.

## What it costs us

Another flag for him to clear on pricing documents that carry quantities.
