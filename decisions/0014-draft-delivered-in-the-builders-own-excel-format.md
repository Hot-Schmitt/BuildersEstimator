## Decision

The first plan: a draft estimate about 80% done, in the builder's own Excel format (his
sheet, his lines, his layout), for him to finish in minutes. The project was a test
bench, not a software build.

## Why

It was the smallest thing that could prove the method: he already works in that
spreadsheet, so nothing new had to be learned or built.

## Considered instead

Nothing else was on the table when this was set; it was the starting plan in version 1.0
of the project instructions. It was replaced the same day by decision 0015, when the
owner asked for a more modern, premium output with Excel as an export only.

## What it costs us

None now; kept as the record of where the project started. His Excel files remain the
source material the estimate is built from.

## His words

None recorded for the original plan. The words that replaced it are in decision 0015.
