## Decision

Locked at 10:45 pm: the laptop estimate screen as its third sketch, redrawn by a design
reviewer, with line item details in a side panel that opens on request and "Add pricing"
as the short label on an unpriced sub bid's price cell. The project's document 15 was
its build source.

## Why

Of Claude's own two laptop sketches, the second passed two of the owner's five tasks and
failed three when a reviewer tested it. The reviewer's version fixed all eight findings,
and the owner locked it.

## Considered instead

**Claude's sketch 1 (three panes all open) and sketch 2 (two panes, details opened in
place).** Both lost to the reviewer's sketch 3.

## What it costs us

The lock stood for fifteen minutes. At 11 pm the owner listed what was wrong with it:
text scrunched and wrapping; nothing drawing the eye; buttons that looked like the
containers; odd wording; no save button; an unclear "Done" and an unclear "Totals and
markup" entry; two headers for one thing; the details column should be a pop-out; and
removing a line item should be on the line item itself. Claude read that as withdrawing
the lock. The root cause was that it had been drawn and reviewed at chat width (decision
0042). Document 15 is kept as a record, not a build source. "Add pricing" later became
"Enter price" (decision 0052).
