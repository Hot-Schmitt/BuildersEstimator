## Decision

Five rows of his sheet that were read as headings (rows 148, 149, 160, 165 and 571)
become lines, through a short list in the sheet's settings block. Part of version 0.5.

## Why

He left those rows empty on Job A, the sheet his line list was made from, so they were
read as headings. The writer cannot carry a decision on a heading: their plan counts sat
in the draft's cover as notes with no source or mark, and they were not lines he could
edit. A High finding in the fresh review of 2026-11-16.

## Considered instead

**Leaving the counts as notes in the cover.** Nothing was dropped silently that way, but
a quantity with no source and no mark breaks the rule that every quantity carries both.

## What it costs us

The line list and the import's line kinds change (the totals do not), so anything keyed
to the old list has to be remade. The list is specific to his sheet.
